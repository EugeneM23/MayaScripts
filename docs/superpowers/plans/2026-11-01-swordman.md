# Swordman Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Put the Max Biped "Sword Man" through our pipeline — our bone names on
his own proportions, an AdvancedSkeleton rig, a retarget that keeps his lengths,
and his sword and shield as catalog weapons on `weapon_r` / `weapon_l`.

**Architecture:** Our 55 bones are created at **his bind**, read from the
skinCluster's `bindPreMatrix`, so the body is **rewired** onto them rather than
re-bound — every vertex stays where it is. Everything else follows the Creep /
Creep 2 road: a source scene, a live AS build in a disposable Maya, two asset
cutters, catalog rows.

**Tech Stack:** mayapy standalone for every build step but the rig; a disposable
GUI Maya on **port 7008** for the AdvancedSkeleton build and the portrait; the
repo's own `maya_scenesetup`, `maya_rigs`, `maya_skeletonmap`, `bonedrive`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-swordman-design.md`

  already exported to `sources/swordman/Swordman_Scene.mb`.

### Task 2: The source — our 55 bones on his bind, the body rewired

**Files:**
- Create: `docs/superpowers/plans/make_swordman_source.py`
- Reads: `sources/swordman/Swordman_Scene.mb`
- Writes: `sources/swordman/Swordman_Source.mb`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the constants block above.
- Produces: a scene holding `|Swordman|root` (55 joints, scale 1, our names),
  `|Swordman|Geometry|Swordman_Body` skinned to it, and
  `|Swordman|Geometry|Swordman_Sword` / `_Shield` at world size parented into
  the weapon bones' frames. Nothing of Max's naming is left.

**The rules, each measured:**

1. **The bind is `bindPreMatrix`, never `dagPose`** (the dagPose leaves the
   palms 3.43 cm off). For each of the 17 influences,
   `bind[leaf] = MMatrix(getAttr("%s.bindPreMatrix[%d]" % (SKIN, i))).inverse()`.
2. **Our joints are created from those world matrices**, parents first, with
   `cmds.joint()` then `xform(ws=True, m=...)`, then `makeIdentity` is NOT used
   — set `jointOrient` from the world matrix and leave `rotate` at zero
   (`joint -e -orientJoint` would move them). Scale stays 1 throughout, so the
   2.54 never enters.
3. **The unskinned six** are laid out as the spec says: read their positions
   from the restored bind pose in a throwaway copy, express each as a fraction
   of the chain's arc length, then re-lay the chain between the two **skinned**
   endpoints keeping his own segment lengths and spreading the endpoint
   difference (0.36 cm over 59 cm for the spine) evenly.
4. **`root`** at `(0, 0, 0)` on the floor with `jointOrient = (-90, 0, 0)`
   (Manny's). The pelvis's world matrix is unchanged; only its parent changes.
5. **The twists** at `TWIST_FRACTION` along each new segment, oriented as their
   parent bone.
6. **`ball_{l,r}`** at the forward-most vertex of the foot's own weights
   (`skinPercent > 0.5` on that foot), projected to `FLOOR_Y`.
7. **The ik helpers**: `ik_foot_root` and `ik_hand_root` under `root` at the
   origin; `ik_foot_{l,r}` on `foot_{l,r}`; `ik_hand_gun` and `ik_hand_r` on
   `hand_r`; `ik_hand_l` on `hand_l`.
8. **`weapon_r`** = `R(SOCKET_TURN).inverse() * W_sword_bind`, so its +Z runs
   down the blade — the UE grip-line convention — and its origin is the sword's
   own origin, inside the grip. **`weapon_l`** is weapon_r's behaviour mirror:
   mirror its local-in-`hand_r` matrix across x and re-express it under
   `hand_l`.
9. **`camera_root` / `camera_bone`** on the midline at the head joint's height,
   `camera_bone` a child of `camera_root` at zero.
10. **`center_of_mass`, `interaction`** under `root` by Manny's locals.
11. **The skin is rewired, not re-bound**: for each influence index `i`,
    disconnect `SKIN.matrix[i]` and connect our joint's `worldInverseMatrix`
    chain exactly as `maya_scenesetup.armor._wear` does; leave `bindPreMatrix`
    alone. Read that function and follow it.
12. **The props carry their WORLD matrices** (trap 86), never their locals.
    They are re-parented under the Geometry group and their points frozen at
    world size; the 2.54 lives in the points from then on.
