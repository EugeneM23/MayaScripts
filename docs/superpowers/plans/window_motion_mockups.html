Speed

1 · now (burst)

Shape unrolls out of the point, rows pop from 30 % with a bounce, the edges race outward lit. In Maya every frame rebuilds a full-window picture: all rows composited, a mask image, the glow, then the translucent window is sent whole to Windows.

2 · zoom from point

The whole window grows from 92 % at the point and fades in, 200 ms; folds to 95 % in 130 ms. One cached picture drawn scaled each frame: the cheapest, nothing to stutter.

3 · soft unroll

The shape unrolls without a bounce, rows fade in and slide 6 px into place nearest first, a thin lit rim fades at the end. One picture plus row offsets, no mask image.

4 · spring

Grows from 70 % at the point with a light spring (one small overshoot, settles by 320 ms). Same cost as 2, with more character.
