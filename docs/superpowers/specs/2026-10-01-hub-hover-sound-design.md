# The hub's hover sound — design (2026-10-01)

The animator: «Давай добавим звуковое сопровождение для нашего интерфейса. Давай для теста сделаем
приятный звук когда срабатывает выделение какого-то элемента. Ну например я вожу мышкой по кнопочкам
нашего меню и вот тут давай сделаем приятный и простой звук наводки на кнопочку».

Asked, and answered:
- **what sounds**: every button of the hub (Recommended) — not only the action buttons, and not the
  grid tiles;
- **which sound**: one sound, chosen by me; the ⋮ menu only switches it on and off.

## Measured first

- Maya 2027 ships `PySide6.QtMultimedia` (`Qt6Multimedia.dll`, the backend
  `plugins/multimedia/windowsmediaplugin.dll`). In mayapy a `QSoundEffect` on a 60 ms 16-bit WAV
  reached `Ready` in **21 ms** and played on the default output (`Speakers (Realtek(R) Audio)`).
  `winsound` (stdlib) is there too.
- The hub's buttons, read off the animator's open hub (Qt meta classes): `QPushButton` (Maya's
  `cmds.button`), `QToolButton` (the jump strip, the header), `QmayaCheckBox` (a `QCheckBox`),
  `QmayaIconTextRadioButton` / `QmayaIconTextCheckBox` (the segments, both `QPushButton`s) — every
  one a **`QAbstractButton`**. The dropdowns are `QmayaOptionMenu` → `QComboBox`; the card headers
  our `CardHead`. The portrait grid, the inventory and the UE list draw their tiles themselves (no
  widget per tile): silent, as chosen.

## What it does

- The mouse **entering** a clickable control of the hub — a `QAbstractButton`, a `QComboBox` or a
  card's header — plays the **hover** sound, if it is enabled. A disabled control is silent; so is
  everything outside the hub's root. Only an Enter counts: moving inside a button, or onto a label
  inside a card header, plays nothing more.
- **⋮ → Interface sounds**, a checkable row, **off by default** (the animator, the same day after
  the build: «Отключи воспроизведение звуков по умолчанию»), remembered in the optionVar
  `skeldarAnimHub_sounds`. Turning it on plays the sound once, so the animator hears what they
  turned on. While it is off no audio is opened at all (the skin preloads the sound only when on).
- The classic hub (frameLayouts, no Qt skin) is silent.

## The sound

Synthesised by our own stdlib code (`maya_hubsound.synth`), shipped as
`SkeldarAnim/assets/sounds/hover.wav` (48 kHz, 16-bit mono) and regenerated by
`docs/superpowers/plans/make_hub_sounds.py`; a test pins the file to the synthesis byte for byte, so
the two cannot drift, and a colleague can swap the file for a sound of their own.

A short soft "glass tick": a sine at E6 (1318.5 Hz) with its octave at a fifth of the level, each
decaying exponentially (16 ms and 7 ms), a 1.5 ms raised-cosine attack (no click at the start), 80 ms
long with a 15 ms cosine fade to exactly zero (no click at the end). Peak −15 dBFS: a hover sound is
heard, not noticed.

## How it plays

`maya_hubsound` (stdlib at import; Qt only inside `play`):
- `play(name)` never raises and answers whether it played. Disabled, a missing file, no audio
  backend: False, silently.
- **QtMultimedia first**: a pool of three `QSoundEffect`s per sound, used in turn, so a quick sweep
  across a row of buttons overlaps instead of cutting the previous tick off (a cut tail clicks).
  Loaded once (`preload`, run when the skin is built, so the first hover is not the one that waits).
- **winsound** where QtMultimedia is missing (Maya before 2025 may lack it): `PlaySound(file,
  SND_FILENAME | SND_ASYNC | SND_NODEFAULT)`.
- **A sweep is throttled**: no new sound within 35 ms of the last one.
- The players live on `sys` (trap 111): an install purges our modules, and a fresh module object
  must not open another three players.

`maya_hubqt.Skin` already watches every Enter in the application (the light that follows the mouse);
`_hover_from` asks `sounding(widget)` (pure Qt, cheap: the type first, then enabled, then whether it
is ours) and calls back `"hover"`. `maya_hub` maps `"hover"` to `maya_hubsound.play("hover")` and
`"sounds"` (the menu row) to `set_enabled`. So `maya_hubqt` knows nothing about audio and
`maya_hubsound` nothing about widgets.

## Not built

A click sound; sounds on the grid tiles and the list rows; a volume control; sounds in the classic
hub. Each is one call to `maya_hubsound.play` away when it is asked for.
